About us

A small shop with strong opinions about coffee.

One espresso machine, one deck oven, one belief • every morning deserves something worth waking up for. This is how we work, every day in Yaba.

From corner space to morning ritual.

We started in a small corner space with a single good espresso machine. Today we roast in micro lots, source from local farms, and laminate croissant dough over 72 hours.

Nothing sits overnight. If it wasn’t baked today, it doesn’t go out.
